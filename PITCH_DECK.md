## Slide 2: The Problem

### African Fleet Operators Are Bleeding Cash

**The cash flow crisis is real:**
- **45-60 day payment cycles** strangle working capital
- Fleet operators wait over a month to get paid for completed jobs
- Meanwhile, fuel, drivers, and maintenance costs don't wait

**Operational inefficiencies compound the problem:**
- **15-20% of kilometers run empty** — wasted fuel, time, and revenue
- Manual quoting leads to margin erosion and lost deals
- No visibility into which routes, drivers, or clients are actually profitable

**Systems don't talk to each other:**
- TMS tracks loads, but not cash flow
- Accounting software doesn't understand fleet operations
- Telematics shows vehicle location, not financial impact

**Result:** Fleet operators are flying blind on profitability while starved for working capital.
